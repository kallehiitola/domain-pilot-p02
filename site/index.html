How many skeins of yarn for a blanket?

Scale total yarn yardage and whole skeins directly from a washed swatch in your actual stitch, then sanity-check against standard blanket sizes and yarn weights.

Quick answer: US throw: a 50 × 60 in blanket scaled from a 4 × 4 in swatch using 12 yd needs 2,475 yd with 10% extra (13 skeins of 200 yd). Metric: a 120 × 160 cm blanket scaled from a 10 × 10 cm swatch using 10 m needs 2,112 m with 10% extra (11 skeins of 200 m).

How the calculation works

Divide finished blanket area by measured swatch area to find how many swatch units make up the blanket. Multiply by yarn consumed in one swatch and by strands held together, add your border/safety allowance, divide by single-skein length, and round up to whole skeins.

What this estimate does not cover

Measure the swatch after the same washing or blocking planned for the blanket. Enter single-strand length in swatch yarn when strands > 1; if your measured swatch length already sums all strands, keep strands at 1. Wide borders, stitch changes, and tension drift alter total yardage.
