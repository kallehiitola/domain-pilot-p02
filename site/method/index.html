Method and assumptions

Blanket yarn and skein calculator

Divide the blanket area by the swatch area, multiply by the yarn used for that swatch, multiply by the number of strands, and add your allowance. Divide by the length in one skein and round up. Measure the swatch after the same washing or blocking you plan for the blanket.

Limits and interpretation

Use the same stitch, tension and yarn as the finished project. Enter yarn use per single strand when using the strands field; if your measured swatch length already totals every strand, leave strands at one. Borders, texture changes and large gauge changes can change the result.

Units and rounding

Lengths convert using exactly 0.3048 metres per foot and 2.54 centimetres per inch. Area and volume conversions use the corresponding squared and cubed factors. Counts round up where whole items must be purchased; wallpaper drops per roll and fabric pieces across a width round down. Displayed values may be rounded, while calculations retain additional precision.

Compare inputs with the worked examples on the calculator page. A different material, layout or measurement can require different assumptions.
