How to how many skeins of yarn for a blanket

Scale yarn use from a measured swatch to your finished blanket dimensions, then round up to whole skeins. A swatch made in the actual stitch is more useful than a generic yarn-weight estimate.

Quick answer: A 120 × 160 cm blanket scaled from a 10 × 10 cm swatch using 10 m needs 2,112 m with 10% extra: 11 skeins of 200 m.

What you need to measure

  • Finished blanket width
  • Finished blanket length
  • Measured swatch width
  • Measured swatch height
  • Yarn used in the swatch
  • Yarn length per skein
  • Strands held together
  • Extra allowance

How to calculate it

Divide the blanket area by the swatch area, multiply by the yarn used for that swatch, multiply by the number of strands, and add your allowance. Divide by the length in one skein and round up. Measure the swatch after the same washing or blocking you plan for the blanket.

What changes the estimate

Use the same stitch, tension and yarn as the finished project. Enter yarn use per single strand when using the strands field; if your measured swatch length already totals every strand, leave strands at one. Borders, texture changes and large gauge changes can change the result.

Worked example

A 120 × 160 cm blanket scaled from a 10 × 10 cm swatch using 10 m needs 2,112 m with 10% extra: 11 skeins of 200 m.
